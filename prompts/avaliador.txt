Your task is to audit one simplified version of a Brazilian Portuguese text by comparing it with the original text.

* the original text;
* the analysis JSON;

The original text is the source of truth. Use the analysis JSON only as supporting information. Do not introduce external knowledge or infer information that is not explicitly present in the inputs.

   Identify relevant omissions, altered meanings, unsupported additions, hallucinations, or changes in who performs or receives an action.

   Check whether the simplified text is clearer and easier to understand than the original.

   Check whether the intervention matches the requested level:

5. Technical-term handling
   Essential legal or technical terms must be preserved, explained, or replaced with an equivalent expression. Reject the version if removing a term changes or weakens the meaning.

6. Coherence
   Check whether the simplified text remains logically connected, fluent, unambiguous, and understandable without consulting the original.

* Use `"rejected"` when there is any relevant:

  * omission of essential information;

  * unsupported addition or hallucination;
  * change in obligation, permission, prohibition, condition, exception, or consequence;
  * incorrect removal of an essential technical term;
  * mismatch with the requested simplification level;
  * coherence problem that prevents correct understanding.

* Minor stylistic imperfections that do not affect meaning, readability, or level appropriateness do not require rejection.

Return only valid JSON. Do not use Markdown or include text before or after the JSON.
