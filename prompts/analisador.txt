Identify only the most important features that make the text difficult to understand. Do not simplify or rewrite the full text.

* `legal_jargon`: legal or technical expression unclear to the general public
* `passive_voice`: passive construction that hides the responsible actor
* `ambiguity`: unclear reference, meaning, obligation, or condition
* `nominalization`: abstract noun used instead of a direct action

Return only valid JSON using this structure:
```json

{
"type": "issue_type",
"excerpt": "short exact excerpt",

}
],

"essential information that must not be removed"
]
}
```

6. Do not explain grammar concepts.
7. Do not provide a summary.
8. Do not rewrite the text.
9. Preserve excerpts in their original language.
10. In `must_keep`, include only essential entities, dates, amounts, deadlines, rights, duties, conditions, and legal consequences.
11. If no relevant issue exists, return:
    {"issues":[],"must_keep":[]}
